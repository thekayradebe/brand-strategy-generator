## The Strategy Frameworks Inside

This isn't prompts wrapped around vague questions. The strategy Claude builds is structured around frameworks used by professional brand strategists:

- **The Four Positioning Questions** — Who precisely, what category, what single point of difference, and why anyone should believe it. These four questions force specificity. Vague answers produce vague strategy. Specific answers produce something ownable.
- **The Positioning Statement Formula** — For [audience] who [problem or desire], [brand] is the [category frame] that [difference] because [reason to believe]. A positioning statement is not a tagline. It's an internal compass. Every content decision gets tested against it.
- **Competitive Whitespace Analysis** — Where is the category under-serving its audience? What do all competitors claim? What assumption does every brand take for granted that this brand could reject? The answer to one of those questions is often where the strongest positioning lives.
- **The Five-Layer Audience Model** — Demographics, psychographics, the problem in the audience's own words, their awareness stage, and the identity-level transformation they're buying into. Most beginner strategies stop at demographics. This one doesn't.
- **The Persona Framework** — Named, specific, human. A primary and a secondary persona with a job, a schedule, an anxiety, and an aspiration. Not a demographic box — a person you can write for.
